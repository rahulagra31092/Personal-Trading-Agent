Earnings Layer
NEW
Pre-Earnings Setup
Is the stock already pricing in a beat (run-up = sell-the-news risk) or a miss (beaten down = surprise pop potential)?
Beat/Miss History + Direction
Historical EPS beat rate per company. Analyst consensus vs implied whisper. Post-earnings 5-day drift direction.
Implied Move (options-implied)
Options market implied ±% move. If actual move exceeds implied = strong continuation signal. If below = reversal risk.
Example output: AMZN earnings May 22 · Beat rate: 78% · Pre-setup: neutral (no run-up) · Implied move: ±5.2% · Recommendation: WATCH — favorable setup if holding through earnings · Post-earnings drift signal activates day+1
8
News Reaction Layer
NEW
News Classification + Historical Reaction
Claude classifies each news item (regulatory, product, macro, insider, partnership). Cross-references with historical: "When GOOG got antitrust news, it dropped -2.8% next day in 7 of 9 cases."
News-Price Divergence Signal
The most powerful signal: stock DROPS on good news = bearish reversal. Stock HOLDS on bad news = bullish strength. These divergences outperform pure sentiment by a wide margin.
Example output: META — Partnership announcement (positive) · Stock reaction: -1.2% (negative divergence) · Historical: similar news = +2.1% avg · Signal: BEARISH — market not believing this news · Confidence: 68%
