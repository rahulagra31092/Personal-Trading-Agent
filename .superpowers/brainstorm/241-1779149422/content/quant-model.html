The Quant Model — Design Section 2 of 4

How raw data becomes a recommendation with confidence bands

1
Technical Indicators Layer
Classic price/volume signals — the foundation of every model
RSI
Overbought/oversold (0–100). >70 = sell signal, <30 = buy signal
MACD
Trend momentum. Signal line crossover = direction change
Bollinger Bands
Price touching lower band = potential bounce. Upper = caution.
EMA 20/50/200
Moving avg crossovers. Golden cross = bullish, death cross = bearish
2
Forecasting Layer (ARIMA + GARCH)
Statistical models for price direction and volatility — where confidence bands come from
ARIMA — Price direction forecast
Looks at historical price patterns to estimate the most likely direction over the next 1–5 days. Outputs: "65% probability up, target $185–$192 range"
GARCH — Volatility forecast
Estimates how wild price swings could get. High GARCH = wider confidence band = more uncertainty. "Expected move: ±4.2% over next 5 days"
3
Smart Money Overlay
Boosts or dampens technical signals based on what large players are doing
13F Filings
Buffett/Ackman/Burry added this stock? +1 conviction boost
ARK Flows
Cathie Wood buying? Adds growth signal for tech/crypto-adjacent
Whale Wallets
Large BTC/ETH/SOL wallet accumulation = crypto buy signal
Congress Trades
Party-in-power member buys before legislation = high-value signal
4
Signal Combiner + Scorer
Weights all signals into a single score per ticker — this is what drives the recommendation
Technical indicators (RSI + MACD + Bollinger + EMA)
35%
ARIMA price direction forecast
25%
Smart money overlay (13F + ARK + Congress + Whale)
25%
News sentiment (positive/negative/neutral)
15%
Final score → BUY (score >0.65) | WATCH (0.4–0.65) | AVOID (<0.4)
5
Monte Carlo Confidence Bands
Runs 1,000 simulations to show the range of outcomes — not just a single number
Example output for AMZN:
Signal: BUY · Confidence: 72%
5-day forecast: $195.40 (base) · Range: $188–$203 (80% CI)
Downside risk: -3.8% · Upside potential: +4.4%
GARCH volatility: moderate (±2.1%/day)
Smart money: 2 of 4 signals confirming
6
Backtester
Before any signal goes live, it's validated on 2 years of historical data
Calculates win rate, Sharpe ratio, max drawdown, and average return per trade for the combined signal model. This is what Month 1 paper trading validates in live conditions.
